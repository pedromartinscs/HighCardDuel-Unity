Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

High Card Arena is developed and published by Nancode.

Information We Collect

High Card Arena does not require account creation and does not ask users to provide personal details such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.

Nancode does not directly collect or store personal profile information from users of High Card Arena.

Local Data Storage

The game stores certain information locally on your device to provide gameplay functionality, including game settings, statistics, progress data, daily claim status, and in-game chip balance.

This local gameplay information remains on your device and is not transmitted to Nancode as personal account data.

Online Features

If online or multiplayer features are available, the game may communicate with Nancode-operated game servers to create or join rooms, synchronize match state, and support gameplay functionality.

These online features do not require users to create an account. Room IDs, match state, connection data, and gameplay messages may be processed temporarily to provide the multiplayer experience.

Advertising

High Card Arena uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to display optional rewarded advertisements.

Rewarded ads are shown only when the user chooses to watch an ad, such as for the Daily Claim reward. Watching a rewarded ad may grant in-game chips, but chips have no real-money value and cannot be cashed out.

Google Mobile Ads may collect and process data such as device identifiers, Advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interaction data, app interaction data, diagnostics, and related information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and service operation.

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Analytics

High Card Arena does not use a separate analytics service operated by Nancode.

However, Google Mobile Ads may process certain data for analytics, ad performance measurement, fraud prevention, and service operation as part of advertising functionality.

Third-Party Services

High Card Arena uses the following third-party service:

Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads — used to provide optional rewarded advertisements.

Google's privacy policy is available at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Security

Data transmitted by Google Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit using modern transport security.

Nancode does not sell personal information and does not operate a user account database for High Card Arena.

Children's Privacy

High Card Arena is intended for users aged 13 and older.

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

No Real-Money Gambling

High Card Arena does not offer real-money gambling, cash-out, real-money prizes, paid chips, or gambling-like purchases.

In-game chips are virtual gameplay items only and have no real-world monetary value.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any future updates will be published on this page.
