Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

High Card Arena is developed and published by Nancode.

Information We Collect

High Card Arena does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information.

The game does not require account creation and does not request personal details such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.

Local Data Storage

The game stores certain information locally on your device to provide gameplay functionality, including game settings, statistics, progress data, and in-game chip balance.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to Nancode.

Advertising

The current version of High Card Arena does not display advertisements.

Future versions may include advertising features. If advertisements are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Analytics

The current version of High Card Arena does not use analytics services.

Third-Party Services

The current version of High Card Arena does not use third-party services that collect user information.

Children's Privacy

High Card Arena is intended for users aged 13 and older.

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
